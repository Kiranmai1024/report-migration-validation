Method: keys are trimmed and lower-cased, the legacy and upgraded outputs are joined on report + period + region + product, and every row is classified exactly once (the row counts reconcile to zero). A value passes if it is identical, within ±0.01 absolute, or within the selected relative tolerance. Reports are ready when the pass rate meets their target (Critical 99.9%, High and Medium 99.5%, Low 99.0%). All data is randomly generated for portfolio purposes.
